human related 

1. driving expertise (independent)
2. reaction time (very small exponential relation to driving expertise)
3. fatigue (independent)
4. decision making (hyperbolic relation to fatigue and linear relation to driving expertise)
5. consistency (linear relation to driving expertise)

autonomous related

0. time tested (negative linear relation to strictness)
1. driving expertise (strictness soft locks it to a minimum value, but linear relation to time tested)
2. reaction time (independent, standardized to 0.2 with very little variance)
3. fatigue (always 0)
4. decision making (linear relation to driving expertise, linear relation to time tested)
5. consistency (linear relation to strictness)

environment related

1. weather (independent)
2. road type (independent)
3. road condition (dependent on weather as well as road type)
4. presence of pedestrians (dependent on road type)
5. presence of animals (dependent on road type)
6. presence of foreign objects on the road (dependent on road type)
7. traffic density (dependent on road type)
